     - B. Circle
     - C. Triangle
     - D. Rectangle
   - **Correct Answer:** C. Triangle
   - **Explanation:** In a square, all angles are 90 degrees. A circle has no angles, and a rectangle has at least one angle of 90 degrees. A triangle has at least one angle that is not 90 degrees.

     - C. 24
